How to Partner

5 Steps to Secure Your Slot

Read the Heal Indira Council Partnership Prospectus 2026 to understand the full scope of benefits, delegate profiles, and city schedules available across our engagement circuit.

Choose your partnership level — Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum — and the city edition you wish to attend. Hospitals may partner in one or multiple cities independently.

Contact Heal Indira for a dedicated partnership discussion. Our team walks you through delegate matching, presentation guidelines, and expected outcomes.

Submit your confirmation and 100% advance payment to reserve your slot. Slots are limited — Platinum only 3, Gold 7, Silver 15, Bronze 30 per city.

Get your onboarding kit, delegate briefing schedule, and presentation guidelines. Your hospital is now ready for curated, high-value B2B engagement.
